# SCEnergy Green Vision

This concept film combines licensed live-action mountain footage with original three-dimensional forest growth, energy storage equipment, and EV charging infrastructure. It illustrates an environmental vision; it is not footage of an SCEnergy installation or a measurement of environmental impact.

## Original work and delivery

421 animated trees with modeled branches and leaf geometry are positioned using optical tracking of the source footage. ESS cabinets, power-conversion equipment, a photovoltaic canopy, chargers, vehicles and an animated energy connection were modeled for this film. A slowed, alternating section of the source camera movement supports the intro and continuous forest scene.

- `green-intro-1080.mp4`: 24-second composition, H.264, 1920 × 1080, 24 fps, no audio.
- `green-idle-1080.mp4`: 8-second continuous forest composition, same format.
- `green-intro-540.mp4` / `green-idle-540.mp4`: 960 × 540 mobile versions.
- `green-start.webp` / `green-forest.webp`: opening and completed-scene posters.

The unmodified source footage is not included in this repository. Reproduction scripts are in `tools/green-vision/`.

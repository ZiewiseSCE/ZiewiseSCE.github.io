# SCEnergy Green Vision

## Current architectural construction film (13 September 2026)

`energy-construction-1080.mp4` and `energy-construction-540.mp4` are original Blender/Cycles architectural renders: foundations, steel, facade, roof PV, ESS, EV charging and forest growth, followed by a moving sun, daytime solar use/storage, and nighttime ESS supply with illuminated SC ENERGY signage. Photographic environment lighting and ground textures come from Poly Haven's **Drakensberg Solitary Mountain** HDRI and **Aerial Rocks 02** material, licensed under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

- [HDRI source](https://polyhaven.com/a/drakensberg_solitary_mountain)
- [Ground material source](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_rocks_02)
- Retrieved and license checked: 2026-09-13.
- 62 seconds, 24 fps, H.264, no audio; 1920×1080 desktop and 960×540 mobile. The gradual day/night cycle is rendered at 12 fps and motion-interpolated to 24 fps; construction retains the original 24 fps.
- `energy-start.webp` and `energy-complete.webp` are renders from the same scene.
- Reproduction scripts and exact asset URLs: `tools/construction-film/`.

This is a concept visualization, not footage of an actual SCEnergy installation. The time-of-day and ESS charge indicators are illustrative operation, not live data. The forest symbolizes an environmental comparison scenario; it is not a tree-planting record. Assumptions are in `tools/construction-film/IMPACT-BASIS.md` and the homepage calculation dialog.

## Original work and delivery

421 animated trees with modeled branches and leaf geometry are positioned using optical tracking of the source footage. ESS cabinets, power-conversion equipment, a photovoltaic canopy, chargers, vehicles and an animated energy connection were modeled for this film. A slowed, alternating section of the source camera movement supports the intro and continuous forest scene.

- `green-intro-1080.mp4`: 24-second composition, H.264, 1920 × 1080, 24 fps, no audio.
- `green-idle-1080.mp4`: 8-second continuous forest composition, same format.
- `green-intro-540.mp4` / `green-idle-540.mp4`: 960 × 540 mobile versions.
- `green-start.webp` / `green-forest.webp`: opening and completed-scene posters.

The unmodified source footage is not included in this repository. Reproduction scripts are in `tools/green-vision/`.

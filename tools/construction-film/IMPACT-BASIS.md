# Environmental comparison — 13 September 2026

The homepage illustrates a solar capacity scenario of approximately 30.9 MW. It does not present a verified installation record or tree-planting record.

## Environmental assumptions

1. Comparison capacity: 30,879.3889 kW, displayed as 30.9 MW.
2. Assumed annual yield: 1,200 kWh/kW/year. Equivalent annual generation: 37,055.2667 MWh/year.
3. Electricity displacement potential: generation × 0.4173 tCO₂e/MWh = 15,463.16278 tCO₂e/year. The factor is the 2023 Korean consumption-side electricity factor announced in December 2025. [Ministry release reproduced by KDI](https://eiec.kdi.re.kr/policy/materialView.do?num=274951&pg=&pp=&topic=P).
4. Pine absorption comparison: annual potential × 1,000 ÷ 6.6 kg CO₂/tree/year = approximately 2,342,903 trees. This compares one year of absorption by 30-year-old average pines. [Korea Climate Change Assessment Report 2014, p.92, Table 4.4](https://www.climate.go.kr/home/cc_data/2015/climate_change_report%28korean%292014_%28effect_adaptation%29.pdf).

These are environmental comparison assumptions, not measured output or achieved emissions reductions. Lifecycle emissions, commissioning dates and actual operating conditions are excluded. The forest is symbolic; it does not count trees actually planted or represent certified offsets. The comparison method is available in Korean, English and Japanese in the homepage dialog.

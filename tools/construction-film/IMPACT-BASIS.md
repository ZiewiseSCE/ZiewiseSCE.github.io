## Assumptions and results

The site owner specified **KRW 180/W = KRW 180,000/kW = KRW 180 million/MW (1.8억 원/MW)** on 13 September 2026. This replaces the earlier draft assumption. It is used as a revenue-to-capacity conversion rate, not asserted as a verified complete-plant EPC price.

1. Capacity equivalent = 5,558,290,000 KRW ÷ 180,000,000 KRW/MW = **30.8793889 MW**, displayed as **30.9 MW**.
2. Hypothetical annual yield = 1,200 kWh/kW/year. Annual generation = 30,879.3889 kW × 1,200 ÷ 1,000 = **37,055.2667 MWh/year**. This is a visualization assumption, not measured output.
3. Electricity displacement potential = annual generation × **0.4173 tCO₂e/MWh** = **15,463.16278 tCO₂e/year**, displayed as **15,463**. This is the 2023 Korean consumption-side electricity factor announced 18 December 2025. [Ministry release reproduced by KDI](https://eiec.kdi.re.kr/policy/materialView.do?num=274951&pg=&pp=&topic=P).
4. Pine absorption equivalent = annual potential × 1,000 ÷ **6.6 kg CO₂/tree/year** = **2,342,903.45 trees**, displayed as **234.3만 그루 상당** in Korean. This is the annual absorption of a 30-year-old average pine from [Korea Climate Change Assessment Report 2014, p.92, Table 4.4](https://www.climate.go.kr/home/cc_data/2015/climate_change_report%28korean%292014_%28effect_adaptation%29.pdf), citing the National Institute of Forest Science (2012).

All included company revenue is hypothetically converted to PV capacity, although the business also includes supply, consulting and other services. The result does not prove actual supply or installation capacity. The carbon figure is annual displacement potential, not cumulative historical abatement; manufacturing/construction lifecycle emissions, actual commissioning dates, locations and operation are not modeled. The tree number compares annual absorption and does not represent actual planting, new seedlings, or a certified offset.

The homepage labels this as an estimated scenario and provides these assumptions in Korean, English and Japanese through “Calculation basis”. No real-time claim is made.
